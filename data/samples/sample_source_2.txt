MODERN PRACTITIONER FIELD NOTES ON NATIVE PADDY SOWING
Author: Green Heritage Farmers Collective
Year: 2021
Location: Nagapattinam District

Field Summary:
Cultivation of organic traditional paddy varieties as practiced by smallholders today.

Standard Sequence:
1. Select mature paddy grains from previous season's storage pot.
2. Cow urine seed treatment: soak paddy seeds overnight in cow urine solution.
3. Broadcast the germinated seeds onto well-plowed wet mud beds.
4. Apply neem oil spray as natural pest deterrent.

Field Notes:
Seedlings emerge within four days with adequate irrigation. Direct broadcasting saves labor compared to conventional machine transplantation.
