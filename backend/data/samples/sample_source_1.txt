TRADITIONAL RICE PRESERVATION & CULTIVATION PROTOCOL
Author: Thanjavur Agricultural Folklore Society
Year: 1968
Region: Cauvery Delta, Tamil Nadu

Introduction:
The preservation of ancestral rice landraces, specifically Mappillai Samba and Karuppu Kavuni, requires strict fidelity to traditional preparatory cycles.

Sequential Procedure:
1. First, select unblemished whole paddy grains harvested during the waxing moon phase.
2. Cow urine seed treatment (Gomutra Vithai Nerthi): soak the selected seeds in a 1:10 dilution of indigenous cow urine for twelve hours.
3. Termite mound clay slurry enrobing: drain the soaked seeds and gently coat them in finely sieved red earth obtained from active termite mounds.
4. Broadcast the germinated seeds onto saturated nursery beds before sunrise.
5. Apply neem cake and fermented green manure to deter soil nematodes.

Observations:
The protective termite clay coating prevents fungal rot during unseasonal rains and enhances early seedling vigor.
